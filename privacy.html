Privacy Policy

Talad Tiles (ตลาดนัดจับสาม) — IzeMonster

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how IzeMonster ("we") handles information in the mobile game Talad Tiles (the "Game") on Android and iOS.

1. What stays on your device

The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address or phone number. Your progress, coins, album and settings are stored only on your device. We run no server that receives them, and they cannot be moved to another device.

2. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one. They are served by Google AdMob, which may collect and process:

  • Advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking)
  • Device information such as model, operating system, language and country
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions, such as ads shown or completed

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Analytics and crash reports

Outside the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the Game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to learn which levels are hard and to fix crashes. They process game events (such as levels started, won or lost, tools used and ads watched), purchase events, device information, an app-instance identifier and crash diagnostics. This data is not used to identify you. In the EEA and UK this collection stays off. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

4. In-app purchase

The Game offers one optional one-time purchase, the Regular's Pack, processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card number or billing details. The Game receives only confirmation that the pack is owned, so it can be restored under Settings.

5. Your choices

  • EEA, UK and other regions where a consent message applies: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears after the first three lessons, before any ad loads. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.
  • iOS: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete your advertising ID or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

6. Children

The Game is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. Data held by Google is kept and secured under Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com. The store keeps your purchase record so the pack can be restored.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) or the GDPR may give you rights over your personal data. We hold no personal data ourselves, but we will help you with any request. Contact us at the address below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes appear on this page with a new date.
